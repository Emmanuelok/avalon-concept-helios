04 / ASK BETTER QUESTIONS

Curiosity is
a renewable resource.

Does 100% mean going off-grid?

No. The model compares annual totals only. Night-time use, seasonal changes and peak demand still matter. Grid independence requires a separate detailed design.

Why does the yield factor change?

Real systems lose energy through temperature, shading, wiring and equipment. Here, a simplified factor lets you explore the effect of those losses.

What does panel surface mean?

This model assigns 2 m² per panel. A real roof needs extra clearance, safe access, structural checks and suitable orientation. This is not a roof feasibility assessment.
